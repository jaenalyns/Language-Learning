🔹Basic Structure of Korean Verbs

Dictionary form: All Korean verbs end in -다 (da) in the dictionary — kind of like Spanish verbs ending in -ar/-er.
Verb Meaning Romanization
가다 to go ga-da
먹다 to eat meok-da
마시다 to drink ma-si-da

Stem = verb minus -다

Verb Stem (meaning) Romanization
가다 가 (go) ga
먹다 먹 (eat) meok
마시다 마시 (drink) ma-si
Endings show:
  • Tense (past, present, future)
  • Politeness (formal/casual)
  • Sentence type (statement, question, etc.)

🕒Present Tense (Polite: –아요 / –어요)

Rule:
• If last vowel of the stem is ㅏ or ㅗ → use -아요 (a-yo)
• Otherwise → use -어요 (eo-yo)
Verb Stem Ending Final Form Romanization English
가다 가 -아요 가요 ga-yo (I/you) go
오다 오 -아요 → 와요 와요 wa-yo (I/you) come
먹다 먹 -어요 먹어요 meok-eo-yo (I/you) eat
마시다 마시 -어요 마셔요 ma-syeo-yo (I/you) drink

🕓Past Tense (Polite: –았어요 / –었어요)

Rule:
• If stem ends in ㅏ or ㅗ → use -았어요 (ass-eo-yo)
• Otherwise → use -었어요 (eoss-eo-yo)
Verb Stem Ending Final Form Romanization English
가다 가 -았어요 갔어요 gat-sseo-yo (I/you) went
오다 오 -았어요 왔어요 wat-sseo-yo (I/you) came
먹다 먹 -었어요 먹었어요 meok-eoss-eo-yo (I/you) ate
마시다 마시 -었어요 마셨어요 ma-syeo-sseo-yo (I/you) drank

🕘Future Tense (Polite: –ㄹ 거예요 / –을 거예요)

Rule:
• If the stem ends in a vowel, use -ㄹ 거예요 (l geo-ye-yo)
• If the stem ends in a consonant, use -을 거예요 (eul geo-ye-yo)
Verb Stem Ends in... Final Form Romanization English
가다 가 vowel 갈 거예요 gal geo-ye-yo (I/you) will go
오다 오 vowel 올 거예요 ol geo-ye-yo (I/you) will come
먹다 먹 consonant 먹을 거예요 meok-eul geo-ye-yo (I/you) will eat
마시다 마시 vowel 마실 거예요 ma-sil geo-ye-yo (I/you) will drink

⚠️When is "-다" Used?

Important Note:

Spanish ✅
Base form used in speech
Quiero comer ("I want to eat")
Korean ❌
Base form NOT used in speech
Not: 먹다 싶어요 ❌
Use: 먹고 싶어요 (meok-go si-peo-yo)
"-다" is ONLY for:
  • Dictionaries
  • Grammar books
  • Formal writing / essays
  • Headlines / signs

💬Want Verb: 싶다 (sip-da)

Verb: 싶다 (sip-da) = "to want (to do something)"
  • You attach -고 싶다 to a verb's -고 form (verb stem + 고)
  • Then conjugate 싶다 for the tense (present, past, future), not the action verb
Key Difference:
  • 먹어요 (meok-eo-yo) = "I eat" (regular verb, polite)
  • 먹고 (meok-go) = "to eat" (used with verbs like "want," "try," etc.)
Common verbs that use the -고 form:
  • 싶다 (want) → 먹고 싶어요 = I want to eat
  • 보다 (try/see) → 먹고 보세요 = Try eating it
  • 있다 (be doing) → 먹고 있어요 = I'm eating
  • 오다 (come after) → 먹고 와요 = Eat and come back
  • 가다 (go after) → 먹고 가요 = Eat and go
  • 자다 (sleep after) → 먹고 자요 = Eat and sleep
  • 놀다 (play after) → 먹고 놀아요 = Eat and play

🕒Present Tense (Polite: -고 싶어요)

Phrase Romanization Meaning
먹고 싶어요 meok-go si-peo-yo I want to eat
가고 싶어요 ga-go si-peo-yo I want to go
자고 싶어요 ja-go si-peo-yo I want to sleep

🕓Past Tense (Polite: -고 싶었어요)

Conjugate 싶다 into past tense → 싶었어요 (sip-eoss-eo-yo)
Phrase Romanization Meaning
먹고 싶었어요 meok-go sip-eoss-eo-yo I wanted to eat
가고 싶었어요 ga-go sip-eoss-eo-yo I wanted to go
자고 싶었어요 ja-go sip-eoss-eo-yo I wanted to sleep

🕘Future Tense (Polite: -고 싶을 거예요)

Conjugate 싶다 into future tense → 싶을 거예요 (sip-eul geo-ye-yo)
Phrase Romanization Meaning
먹고 싶을 거예요 meok-go sip-eul geo-ye-yo I will want to eat
가고 싶을 거예요 ga-go sip-eul geo-ye-yo I will want to go
자고 싶을 거예요 ja-go sip-eul geo-ye-yo I will want to sleep

🧠Summary Table (Using 먹다 = to eat)

Tense Korean Phrase Romanization English Translation
Present 먹고 싶어요 meok-go si-peo-yo I want to eat
Past 먹고 싶었어요 meok-go sip-eoss-eo-yo I wanted to eat
Future 먹고 싶을 거예요 meok-go sip-eul geo-ye-yo I will want to eat
✅ So in short:

🔹 먹어요 (meok-eo-yo) = "I eat" → This is a conjugated verb, polite present tense, used by itself as a statement.

🔹 먹고 (meok-go) = "to eat" → This is a connector, not a final verb. It's used with other verbs like:
  • 싶어요 (want) → 먹고 싶어요 = I want to eat
  • 보세요 (try) → 먹고 보세요 = Try eating
  • 있어요 (doing) → 먹고 있어요 = I'm eating

⚠️ Don't confuse them!

One finishes a sentence, the other connects to the real verb you're trying to use.
