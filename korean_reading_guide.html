Memory Tip: Each consonant is organized into one of the consonant and vowel systems. Just like other languages, you need to memorize the shape of each letter as a symbol for every sound possible.
Special Note about ㅇ: When ㅇ is at the beginning of a character it's nothing (silent), and when it's at the end it makes an "ng" sound.
Aspirated Consonants: ㅋ becomes "k" and ㅌ becomes "t" - kind of like an addition to ㄱ → g and ㄷ → d. These are the voiced counterparts of the "aspirated" (breathy) versions of the basic consonants.

How Korean Syllables Work

Each syllable is squished into one box of one consonant and one or two vowel:

Just like building blocks! You put the letters together as a bundle!

밤 bam
북 book

Let's kick it up a notch!

You can add a 3rd consonant after two vowels by adding a bottom consonant:

밫 bam + t
꽃 kkot
