Sino-Korean Numbers

When to Use Each Number System

Sino-Korean Numbers (中)

  • Dates (years, months, days)
  • Money and prices
  • Phone numbers
  • Addresses
  • Mathematical calculations
  • Time (minutes and seconds)
  • Floor numbers
  • Large numbers (100+)
  • Academic/formal contexts

Native Korean Numbers (純)

  • Counting objects (1-99)
  • Age of people
  • Time (hours)
  • Counting people
  • Small quantities
  • Traditional contexts
  • With counters (개, 마리, 명)
  • Ordinal numbers (first, second)
  • Casual conversation
